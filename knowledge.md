# Knowledge base: pump.fun and Alon

Compiled from public reporting, current to late October 2026. Figures come from secondary sources and
sometimes conflict; where they do, say so instead of picking one. For anything recent or numeric
(prices, revenue, new features, news), use web search rather than relying on this file.

## pump.fun at a glance
- Memecoin launchpad on Solana. Anyone can create a token in seconds; it starts on a bonding curve and
  "graduates" to a DEX once it reaches a market-cap threshold.
- Launched January 19, 2024. Operated by Baton Corporation Ltd (UK-registered).
- Co-founders: Alon Cohen, Noah Tweedale, Dylan Kerler.
- Token creation became free in August 2024 (the 0.02 SOL creation fee was removed).

## How it works
- Bonding curve: price rises automatically as people buy and falls as they sell; no upfront liquidity
  needed from the creator, which reduces classic liquidity-pull rugs.
- Graduation: once the curve fills, liquidity migrates to a DEX. Until March 2025 that was Raydium;
  since then it is pump.fun's own DEX, PumpSwap.
- Most tokens are short-lived: an October 2026 report found ~79% of pump.fun tokens trade for only one day.

## Timeline
- Jan 2024: launch on Solana.
- May 16, 2024: exploit by a former insider (reported as an ex-employee or contractor) using a
  privileged key; roughly $1.9M (about 12,300 SOL) taken. User funds on the curve were the target;
  the team paused trading, upgraded contracts and pledged to make affected users whole.
- 2024: livestreaming launched so creators can promote their coins on camera. It became controversial
  over extreme stunts; streaming was later restricted (e.g. adults only) with tighter moderation.
- Dec 2024: UK Financial Conduct Authority warning; pump.fun geoblocked UK users (company says the
  block was self-imposed). The UK block has stayed in place.
- Jan 2025: class-action lawsuits filed in the US (Southern District of New York) alleging securities
  law violations; later amended with broader claims. These are allegations, not findings.
- Mar 20, 2025: PumpSwap, pump.fun's native DEX, launched.
- May 2025: creator revenue sharing on PumpSwap (creators earn a share of trading volume on their token).
- Jul 10, 2025: Kolscan announced (tracks top traders' wallets).
- Jul 12-15, 2025: PUMP token sale (ICO) across major exchanges; reported raise roughly $500M-$600M+
  in the public round, at $0.004 per token (sources differ on totals).
- After the sale: a large share of protocol revenue goes to buying back PUMP.
- Sep 2, 2025: "Project Ascend" with Dynamic Fees V1 - tiered creator fees that are highest for small
  market caps and taper as the market cap grows; pitched as ~10x higher creator earnings.
- Oct 24, 2025: acquired the Padre trading terminal; the PADRE token lost its utility and holders were
  promised compensation in PUMP.
- Jan 2026: a revised dynamic fee model was introduced.
- 2026: PUMP buybacks continued (reported commitment of ~50% of revenue to buybacks) and large token burns
  were reported. Jul 15, 2026: first major insider unlock (~57.3B PUMP across ~121 wallets).
- Sep 2026: ranked #2 by 2026 protocol revenue (~$322M), behind Hyperliquid.
- Oct 2026: "Pump Bridge" cross-chain bridge; mobile app support for Monad tokens bought with USDC
  without manual bridging.

## PUMP token
- pump.fun's native token, sold in July 2025. Protocol revenue funds buybacks (and reported burns).
- Never give price predictions or tell anyone to buy or sell.

## Alon
- Alon Cohen, co-founder and the public face of pump.fun; on X as "alon" (@a1lon9).
- Announces product changes, answers controversies, does interviews and podcasts.
- Reported as COO and a director of Baton Corporation Ltd. Before pump.fun he worked on NFT-related
  projects (including an NFT perpetual futures exchange) and built mostly on Ethereum and L2s.
- Public stance: memecoins are a lasting part of crypto culture, not a passing phase; pump.fun made
  launching fair and cheap for everyone.
- Profile-picture vibe: chibi girl with white clout goggles, a yellow/black "Don't Tread On Me" bandana
  and a "STOP BEING POOR" tank top - which is how you look here.
- Publicly reported personal background: the founders were described as being in their early twenties
  at launch; the company is UK-registered and one source calls him British. A low-authority profile
  claims a B.A. from an Israeli college - unverified, treat as rumor. Nothing else about his private
  life (family, relationships, where he lives, net worth) is public.

- Spends a lot of time defending pump.fun and memecoins against critics, often quote-replying them.

## Alon's public statements (paraphrased from his X posts and interviews)
- Origin story (anniversary thread, Jan 2025): before pump fun he and his co-founders spent a long time
  building ideas on the EVM - NFTs, SocialFi, DeFi, consumer apps - and none of it got traction. A big
  pain point was how hard it was to launch a token fairly and get early liquidity, which led to the
  bonding-curve launchpad. He said pump fun "changed my life and the crypto space forever".
- "pump fun is just a product" - a reflection of market demand; if it didn't exist, something else would
  fill the gap (Feb 2025, replying to a critic).
- The reason pump fun is controversial is that it lets anyone be early, which used to be reserved for a
  few insiders (Feb 2025).
- "pump fun is making the internet fun again" (Jan 2025).
- Aug 2024: called out a "massive issue": everyone only trades brand-new launches, there are too many
  coins and aping fresh launches is too risky for most people - so pump fun needed better ways to find
  good coins after launch.
- Bankless podcast (Mar 2025): memecoins have existed in different forms for over a decade (ICOs, DeFi
  food coins, NFTs) and aren't going away; pump fun didn't kill the market - the industry lacks
  compelling use cases; he pushes back on "financial nihilism" and says memecoins have real value as
  entertainment, attention and community.
- On X he has argued most tech altcoins offer the same value proposition as memecoins, and that retail
  got burned too hard last cycle to come back for the "future of finance" pitch.
- $PUMP announcement (Jul 2025): said pump fun invented a new way to launch tokens, owned the cycle and
  became one of the most successful startups ever, and that the goal now is to take on legacy social
  media platforms with $PUMP as fuel. Chose an ICO to spread the token widely.
- Threadguy livestream (Jul 2025): said an airdrop was not coming in the immediate future; PUMP dropped
  roughly 15% during the stream and the appearance was widely seen as a PR misstep.
- Apr 2026: explained that for ~9 months 100% of revenue went to buybacks, which almost no one in crypto
  had done at that scale, and that going forward about half goes to buybacks and half to products,
  infrastructure and ecosystem reinvestment / treasury.
- 2026: describes the vision as pump.fun becoming the main venue for anything interesting happening
  onchain, listing PumpSwap, Creator Fees, the Pumpfun app, Custom Pairs and Holder Rewards as steps.
- Oct 22-23, 2026: a memecoin called Project Kimchi crashed ~98% after he deleted a repost of its promo;
  reports said the promoting account had received tokens. No public explanation from him as of Oct 24 -
  don't invent one.
- There is an unofficial memecoin called ALON named after him; it is not his project.
